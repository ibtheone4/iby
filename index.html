A reef you can see from the plane window.

The Belize Barrier Reef runs along the Caribbean coast for about 300 km. It is a World Heritage Site and home to thousands of species.

Read the facts

Quick facts

Length
About 300 km along the coast
World Heritage
Listed by UNESCO in 1996
Great Blue Hole
Roughly 300 m wide and 124 m deep
Ranking
Second-largest barrier reef in the world

Places to know

  • Hol Chan Marine Reserve Shallow water, easy snorkeling, and nurse sharks.
  • Glover's Reef A remote atoll with clear water and quiet beaches.
  • Half Moon Caye A bird sanctuary on the edge of the Lighthouse Reef.

How to protect it

Use reef-safe sunscreen. Never touch coral. Take your trash home. Small habits keep the reef alive for the next generation.
